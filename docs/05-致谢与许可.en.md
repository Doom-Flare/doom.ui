## What this repo does not contain

- Any Minecraft / Mojang assets or code; nor any decompiled Minecraft output.
- Third-party datapacks used as development references: they were used only to compare behaviour and are not distributed with this repo.
- The real-machine acceptance bench and the per-round evidence reports: that setup needs a server with RCON open plus a real client, and it lives in a private development repo, not here.
  What this repo keeps is the part you can re-run yourself (the three gates in `src/tools/`).
